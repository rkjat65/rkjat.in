My Approach to AI

On using AI as a tool

⚡

I use AI to move faster — not to think less.

I'm transparent about this: I use AI tools to help write code, accelerate data processing, and prototype visualizations faster. But AI doesn't know whether India's fiscal devolution formula is equitable. It doesn't know which Lok Sabha trends matter. It doesn't know how to frame an insight for a policymaker. That judgment comes from years of study, reading, and thinking — and that's mine.

Think of it like a good architect who uses CAD software. The software executes the drawing. The architect decides what to build, and why. In 2025, an analyst who knows how to use AI effectively gets more done, with more depth, than one who doesn't. That's not a shortcut — it's a skill.
