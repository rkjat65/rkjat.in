What this file contains

A normalized snapshot of ten AI companies across eleven parameters:

Field Meaning
company Legal or commonly used brand name
type Business model category (foundation lab, big tech, enterprise, infrastructure)
headquarters Primary HQ city
est_revenue_usd_bn Estimated AI-related annual revenue in USD billions (0 where revenue is internal/unreported)
total_funding_usd_bn Cumulative venture funding in USD billions (0 for big-tech internal groups)
employees Approximate headcount in AI-related roles
api_adoption_index 0-100 proxy for developer and API adoption
research_impact_index 0-100 proxy for benchmark and citation influence
compute_spend_index 0-100 proxy for relative compute spend
open_models_count Count of openly released model families
enterprise_deals_index 0-100 proxy for Fortune 500 / government contract penetration

Important disclaimer

This is an illustrative teaching dataset compiled for data storytelling on rkjat.in. Figures blend public reporting, industry estimates, and normalized indices. It is not investment advice and should not be used for trading or valuation without primary-source verification.
