Project Overview

This comprehensive analytics dashboard provides deep insights into India's performance during the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026. The project analyzes match data, batting patterns, bowling strategies, player statistics, and team dynamics across 9 matches.

📊 Dashboard Features

Match Results

Detailed scorecards and analysis of all 9 matches played by India, including venue, date, player of the match, and match outcomes.

Batting Analytics

India runs per match, cumulative scoring trends, and performance metrics across the tournament duration.

Bowling Insights

Bowling economy rates, wicket analyses, and strategic dismissal patterns of Indian batsmen.

Phase Analysis

Comprehensive breakdown of powerplay, middle overs, and death overs performance.

Player Statistics

Individual player performance metrics including batting averages, strike rates, and bowling figures.

Fielding & Partnerships

Fielding positions, fall of wickets (FOW), and key partnerships that shaped match outcomes.
