How I approach the work

Questions before tools.

01

Start with a useful question

The subject and the audience come first. The right chart or interface follows from knowing what someone needs to understand.

02

Keep the context

Numbers need sources, definitions, and time periods. A clear explanation makes the limits of a comparison visible.

03

Make it approachable

Whether it's a learning deck, an essay, or a dashboard, the goal is to help someone follow the idea and explore further.

On using AI

Tools help me build.
Judgment stays with me.

I use AI tools to support coding, research, data processing, and visual production. I remain responsible for the questions, source checks, interpretation, and the final experience.

I'm interested in collaborations around research, content, data, education, and products that make knowledge more useful.
