Project Overview

This interactive visualization explores India's fiscal federalism system, analyzing how central tax revenue is collected from states and redistributed through devolution. The analysis covers FY 2020-21 to 2024-25, revealing fascinating patterns in tax contributions and distributions across Indian states.

Understanding Fiscal Federalism

India's fiscal federalism is a sophisticated system designed to balance regional disparities. States with strong industrial bases and higher GDP contribute significantly to the central tax pool. These funds are then redistributed to ensure that developing states have the resources needed for infrastructure, education, and healthcare development. This analysis visualizes this complex system in an accessible, interactive format.
