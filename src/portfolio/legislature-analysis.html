Project Overview

This comprehensive analysis examines the Indian legislature through data-driven insights, profiling Members of Parliament across multiple dimensions including educational qualifications, asset declarations, criminal case involvement, and party affiliations. The interactive dashboard enables citizens, researchers, and journalists to explore patterns in Indian democracy with unprecedented transparency.

Analysis Dimensions

  • Educational Profiles: Breakdown of MPs by education levels, from graduates to PhDs, across parties
  • Asset Analysis: Distribution of declared assets, identifying trends in wealth across political spectrum
  • Criminal Cases: Transparent view of pending criminal cases against MPs, categorized by severity
  • Party Dominance: Analysis of party representation, seat distribution, and voting patterns
  • Demographic Insights: Age distribution, gender representation, and regional patterns
  • Historical Trends: Election-over-election comparison of key metrics

Dashboard Features

The interactive dashboard provides powerful analytical tools:

  • Party Dominance Visualization

    Interactive charts showing seat share, vote percentage, and regional strength of major political parties. Compare performance across states and constituencies.

  • Criminal Cases Analysis

    Detailed breakdown of pending cases against MPs, categorized by type (serious/non-serious), party affiliation, and state. Includes year-over-year trend analysis.

  • Education Level Distribution

    Comprehensive view of educational qualifications of MPs, with filters by party, state, and term. Visualize correlations between education and policy focus areas.

  • Assets Distribution Explorer

    Interactive wealth distribution charts showing declared assets of MPs. Compare across parties, identify top asset holders, and analyze wealth concentration patterns.

    • Transparency in Democracy

    This project embodies the principle of democratic transparency by making legislature data accessible and understandable. By visualizing complex political data, it empowers citizens to make informed decisions and hold elected representatives accountable.
