How I Built GyanGram as a Non-Coder

I did not start as an engineer. I started as someone who understood the learner.

Radhakishan Jat/20 January 2026/7 min read
EducationTechnologyIndiaProduct
ShareXLinkedIn

GyanGram began as a product hypothesis, not a tech resume move: competitive exam learners scroll constantly, but rarely retain.

I had six years of UPSC preparation, three Prelims clears, and direct classroom experience with MPPSC aspirants. The insight was behavioral. The build challenge was execution.

Constraint

Solo builder, limited engineering background, high UX expectations from learners used to polished consumer apps.

What I chose to optimize

  1. Retention over raw content volume
  2. Structured revision loops over passive reading
  3. Credible sourcing over hype features

Flutter made cross-platform delivery realistic. AI tools accelerated implementation, but product judgment, syllabus mapping, and learner empathy stayed human.

Key takeaway

Non-coder does not mean non-builder. It means you must be ruthless about scope and honest about tradeoffs.
