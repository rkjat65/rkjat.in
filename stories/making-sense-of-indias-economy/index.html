Making Sense of India's Economy

India's economy is not one number. It is a stack of indicators, institutions, and regional realities.

Radhakishan Jat/15 March 2026/8 min read
IndiaEconomyPolicyData Viz
ShareXLinkedIn

India’s macro story is often reduced to a GDP headline. That headline matters, but it rarely answers the question a reader actually has: what changed, for whom, and why should I care?

The editorial question

Good economic storytelling starts with a decision or debate, not a dataset.

What I look at first

Before charts, I map three layers:

  1. Growth and prices (GDP, inflation, employment proxies)
  2. Fiscal and external balance (deficit, debt, trade, reserves)
  3. Structural composition (agriculture, industry, services, investment)

That framing shaped my India’s Economic Pulse dashboard and several policy explainers.

Key takeaway

Macro indicators only become stories when you connect them to institutions, timing, and regional variation.

A simple illustration

The chart below is a teaching snapshot, not a policy forecast. It shows why small multiples and labeled sources beat one dramatic number.

Why this matters for readers

Policymakers, students, journalists, and founders all encounter the same problem: trustworthy public data exists, but the narrative layer is missing. My work tries to supply that layer without overselling certainty.

If you are building similar work, document sources, show your transformations, and write for a smart non-technical reader. That is the bar I use across this site.
